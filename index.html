Desmos Mastery Course Free

FREE

Master the Desmos graphing calculator the most underused tool on the SAT. This course used to cost free. It's now completely free.

1

The Desmos Interface

Learn what Desmos can do beyond just graphing lines find zeros, intersections, and values instantly without any algebra.

2

Zeros, Intersections & Systems

Solve systems of equations in seconds by graphing both equations and reading the intersection. Works for linear, quadratic, and exponential systems.

3

Quadratics & Vertex

Find the vertex, roots, and axis of symmetry of any quadratic instantly. Includes the trick for "symmetric about x = k" SAT problems.

4

Regression & Scatterplots

Run linear and exponential regressions in Desmos to answer scatterplot questions in 15 seconds instead of guessing.

5

Exponential Models

Graph exponential functions to find percent change, half-life, and doubling time the patterns the SAT uses most in Advanced Math.

6

Sliders & Dynamic Problems

Use Desmos sliders to understand parametric problems "for what value of k does the system have no solution" becomes a 10-second visual check.

7

Full Desmos Exam Simulation

40 SAT problems designed specifically for Desmos. Time yourself. Compare Desmos time vs. algebraic time on each one.
